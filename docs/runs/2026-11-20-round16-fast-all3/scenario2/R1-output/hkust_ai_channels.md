## 3. Email / bulletin digest (students and staff)

**Subject: Free homegrown AI is now on campus — what it means for your coursework**

Dear students and colleagues,

HKUST announced on 22 April 2025 that all staff and students can use HKGAI V1, Hong Kong's first homegrown large language model, at no cost — the first local university to do so, according to the university.

Three things to know:
1. **Rules are course-level.** Since Fall 2023-24, each instructor sets the GenAI policy for their course and must communicate it in writing. Check your course outline; if it is unclear, ask.
2. **Use the university platform for sensitive work.** Chat data on the HKUST GenAI Platform are protected and visible only to you; do not put restricted data into external AI tools without authorisation.
3. **Integrity rules still apply.** AI-fabricated references count as plagiarism, and academic misconduct need not be intentional.
