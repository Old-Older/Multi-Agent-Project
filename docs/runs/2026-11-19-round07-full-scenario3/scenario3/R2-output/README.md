**Audience / scope (confirmed with you):** internal investment committee, numbers-heavy, ends in a concrete stance with risks.

**Structure:** 1 title · 2 five judgments · 3 the 2026 price cycle (WSTS) · 4 2027 deceleration and the DRAM/NAND split · 5 valuation · 6 supply and policy · 7 sub-segment stance (three overweights conditional, half-size until verified) · 8 implementation and vehicles · 9 risks · 10 decision requested.

**Core thesis:** 2026 was a memory price cycle (industry +89.9% to $1.51tn; memory +249.5% to $803.9bn, i.e. 53% of revenue — that share is an Axis Intelligence derivation, tagged UNVERIFIED on the slide), not a volume cycle. 2027 slows to ~+27% and splits (DRAM tight, NAND easing 2H27). The SOX at 13.6× forward after a −34.1% drawdown is cheap only if 2027 estimates hold — so the risk is earnings, not the multiple.

**Stance:** neutral-to-modest overweight. Foundry/packaging, semicap and AI networking are overweights **only on condition** — held at half the normal active weight until TSMC advanced-node share and CoWoS/HBM capacity are verified, reverting to neutral if that check fails. Neutral on NVDA; neutral on DRAM/HBM with a trim into strength. Underweight NAND and consumer/analog/auto. Sized for a 50%+ drawdown.

**Verification status:** most figures are sourced and dated, but the deck also carries figures tagged DERIVED, ESTIMATE or UNVERIFIED — the 53% memory share (third-party derivation), the +24.8% ex-memory growth (derived from WSTS totals), and the ~5 bp UCITS withholding saving (estimate on unverified yields). Explicitly excluded as unverified: TSMC advanced-node share, CoWoS and HBM capacity by supplier, 2026 Entity List additions, SMIC/Huawei progress, CHIPS/EU/Japan subsidy totals, UCITS TERs/ISINs, any 2028 forecast, the outcome of the 1 Jul 2026 Section 232 data-centre review, and the 30% US dividend withholding rate (statutory, treaty-dependent — confirm with tax counsel). Also not retrieved and therefore not used: S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 total GICS semiconductor sector weights, and the SOX trailing P/E (only the forward multiple is sourced). WSTS Autumn 2026 publishes 1 Dec 2026 and will need to be folded in.
