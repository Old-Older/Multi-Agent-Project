Semiconductors in 2027:
a price cycle, not a volume cycle

What the 2026 boom actually was, why the sector trades at 13.6× forward after a −34% drawdown, and the stance we recommend for 2027.

Prepared by the fund manager with research, risk and marketing input. All figures sourced and dated; unverified items are labelled UNVERIFIED on the slide where they appear.
Executive summary

Five judgments

1. 2026 was a memory price shock

WSTS: market $1.51tn in 2026, +89.9%; memory alone $803.9bn, +249.5% — 53% of the industry. Strip memory and growth is +24.8% ($565.6bn → ~$706bn) DERIVED.

2. 2027 decelerates, and splits

WSTS 2027: ~$1.9tn, +27%. TrendForce: DRAM tight through 2027, NAND eases in 2H27. The first crack is NAND, not DRAM.

3. Valuation is not the risk; earnings are

SOX forward P/E 13.6× (0th pct of 5 yrs, 9th since 2002) after −34.1% from the 22 Jun 2026 high. Cheap only if 2027 estimates hold.

4. Policy has become a cost line, not a headline

Section 232: 25% on defined advanced chips from 15 Jan 2026, data-centre end-uses exempt; Phase 2 broad tariff recommended. Nov 2026 BIS rules shift controls to customer verification.

5. Stance: neutral-to-modest overweight, barbell

Own foundry, equipment and networking; keep memory at or below benchmark; size for a 50%+ drawdown (SOX fell 87.2% in 2000–08).

What would change our mind: 2027 CSP capex guidance cut below +30%; DRAM contract prices turning negative; a Taiwan or broad-tariff shock.
Sources: WSTS Spring 2026 forecast (2 Jun 2026); TrendForce (30 Jul 2026); historyofmarket.com & dollarliquidity.com (17 Nov 2026); EY Tax News (15 Jan 2026); TrendForce (18 Nov 2026).

Supply & policy

Policy is now a cost line and a compliance tax

Section 232 — verified

  • Proclamation signed 14 Jan 2026; 25% ad valorem effective 15 Jan 2026 on advanced computing chips meeting specific TPP/DRAM-bandwidth thresholds (HTSUS 8471.50/8471.80/8473.30).
  • US data-centre, R&D, startup and repair end-uses exempt; no drawback; FTZ privileged foreign status.
  • Phase 2: Commerce recommended broader tariffs at a "significant" rate after negotiations, with a tariff-offset programme for domestic investment.
  • 1 Jul 2026 data-centre review trigger — outcome UNVERIFIED.

Export controls — Nov 2026

  • BIS narrowed foundry/OSAT licence exemptions and tightened customer verification for TSMC, Samsung, ASE, Amkor.
  • Immediate supply impact judged limited: advanced AI packaging remains fully utilised.

Risks

What breaks the thesis

1. Estimate risk (highest)

2–4× forward P/E on memory and 11× on NVDA are only cheap if 2027 estimates hold. A 30–50% EPS cut turns "cheap" into "fair".

2. Concentration

Top-10 = 61–69% of the cap-weighted vehicles; NVDA+TSM+AVGO = 34% of SMH. A single-customer or Taiwan shock is a fund-level event, not diversifiable inside the sleeve.

3. Taiwan / cross-Strait

TSM is 10.1% of SMH and 4.3% of SOXQ. No verified share statistic was retrieved, but sold-out advanced packaging is the verified evidence of dependence.

4. Policy

Section 232 Phase 2 broad tariff; the 1 Jul 2026 data-centre review outcome is unconfirmed; Nov 2026 BIS customer-verification rules add lead time.

5. Demand destruction

Notebooks −9.4% in 2026; iPhone BOM +38%; reballed memory at 7–9% brand penetration in 2027. High memory prices are now taxing the end market that funds the cycle.

6. Drawdown history

SOX fell 87.2% peak-to-trough (Mar 2000–Nov 2008) and took 17 years to recover; −48% in 2008 alone. Position sizing must assume a 50%+ drawdown is possible.

7. Data risk in this deck

WSTS Autumn 2026 publishes 1 Dec 2026; no 2028 forecast exists; TSMC share, CoWoS/HBM capacity, Entity List and subsidy figures are unverified and excluded from the numbers above.

Recommendation

Decision requested

We recommend

  • Neutral-to-modest overweight semiconductors versus the benchmark, entered after a −34% de-rating rather than at the June high.
  • Barbell: conditionally overweight foundry, semicap and AI networking (half-size until verified); neutral accelerators and DRAM; underweight NAND and consumer analog.
  • Structure: core index (SOXQ/SOXX) + PSI satellite + 3 toll-road names; review UCITS alternatives for non-US holders.
  • Size for a 50% drawdown and set a hard stop on the thesis, not the price.

What we would need to go to full overweight

WSTS Autumn 2026 confirming 2027 ≥ +27%; CSP 2027 capex guidance ≥ +50%; DRAM contract prices still positive in 1Q27.

What we would need to go underweight

Any one of: 2027 CSP capex guidance cut below +30%; DRAM contract prices turning negative; Section 232 Phase 2 broad tariff; a cross-Strait escalation.

Open items before the next IC

  • Verify TSMC advanced-node share and CoWoS/HBM capacity (TrendForce quarterly).
  • Confirm Section 232 Phase 2 status and the 1 Jul 2026 review outcome.
  • Confirm UCITS TERs/ISINs on issuer KIIDs.
  • Refresh with the WSTS Autumn 2026 forecast (1 Dec 2026).
Bottom line: the 2026 boom was a memory price cycle. 2027 is a slower, more selective year — and the sector's low multiple is a bet on estimates, not a margin of safety.
