## Central thesis (must be visible on slide 2 and repeated in the conclusion)
1. 2026 was a **memory price cycle wearing an AI costume**: Gartner has memory at 54% of a US$1.6trn 2026 market; JPMorgan shows Aug-2026 ASPs +24% MoM with units −1% MoM. Ex-memory growth is ~+37% (2026), +21% (2027), +16% (2028).
2. AI compute demand is still accelerating (NVIDIA Q3 FY27 DC $107.6bn +110% YoY; Q4 guide $130bn with zero China DC), but the **funding model has changed**: big-4 capex $202bn vs $175bn operating cash flow in Q3-2026 = $1.15 per $1 (vs $0.77 a year earlier).
3. The sector is **cheap on consensus, not cheap on risk**: SOX 13.0x forward P/E (FactSet, 5 Nov 2026) vs ~19.7x 10-yr average, but that is because the market discounts the 2027 EPS number itself (LSEG: semis EPS +125% 2026E, +65% 2027E).
4. The 2026 drawdown (−38.5% from the 22 Jun 2026 peak, 136 days) is **large but young** vs history (−32% average over 253 days across the last four cycles; 2000–02 tail case −85%).
5. Therefore: **own the earnings, not the beta** — barbell of NVDA/TSMC/AVGO + a sized memory sleeve + ASIC/packaging supply chain; avoid consumer-levered semis; stage entries; size by confidence in the 2027 capex number.
