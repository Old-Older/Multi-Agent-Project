## What this means (5 bullets)

1. **The 2026 "record market" is a memory price cycle wearing an AI costume.** Gartner has memory at 54% of a $1.6trn market (1 Sep 2026) and JPMorgan shows Aug-2026 ASPs +24% MoM with units −1% (5 Oct 2026). Underwriting 2027–28 earnings on 2026 revenue growth rates would be a category error: ex-memory growth is ~37%/21%/16% (JPMorgan), and TrendForce already has DRAM contract price growth decelerating to 0–5% QoQ by H2-2027 (18 Nov 2026).
2. **AI compute demand is still accelerating, but the funding model has changed.** NVIDIA guided Q4 FY27 to $130bn with zero China DC revenue (18 Nov 2026) and AMD DC revenue nearly doubled (3 Nov 2026), yet the big four spent $1.15 of capex per $1 of operating cash flow in Q3-2026 vs $0.77 a year earlier (Trefis, 5 Nov 2026). Watch free-cash-flow trough (consensus: 1H-2027) and any shift to debt/off-balance-sheet financing as the first crack.
3. **Custom silicon is the structural share loser for merchant GPU, but a margin tailwind for Broadcom/Marvell and TSMC.** Custom ASICs grew ~44.6% vs merchant GPUs ~16.1% in 2026 (⚠ secondary), Broadcom is credited with ~60% of the custom market by 2027 (Counterpoint, Jan 2026). Position for the ASIC supply chain, not only the GPU leader.
4. **HBM is the tightest node in the system and the pricing power sits with the memory makers into 2027.** HBM4 at $3.00–5.50/Gb in 2027 (1.5–2.7× YoY), ~30% of DRAM wafer input, 2027 output largely pre-sold (TrendForce, 18 Nov 2026). The key risk is *supplier rotation*: TrendForce sees Samsung retaking #1 in 2027 — a share-shift trade within memory, not just a beta trade.
5. **Consumer end markets are being taxed by the AI build-out.** PC −10.4% and smartphone −8.4% in 2026 (Gartner, 26 Feb 2026), smartphone SoCs −21% YoY in 3Q-2026 (Counterpoint, 5 Nov 2026), auto recovery pushed to 2027 (BNP Paribas, 23 Mar 2026). Avoid consumer-levered semis (PC/phone-driven MCU, analog, driver IC) and prefer industrial restocking + AI infrastructure exposure.

## ⚠ Could not verify / caveats

- **No verified 2028–2030 total-market number** from WSTS, Gartner or IDC. Only JPMorgan's 2028 $2.93trn (5 Oct 2026), Gartner's 2030 AI-DC mix (>53%) and IDC's 2030 data-center semis ($843.2bn) are sourced. Treat any 2030 total as an extrapolation.
- **WSTS Autumn 2026 forecast not directly retrieved.** The GSA tracker shows WSTS Aug-2026 at +108.1% for 2026 and +29.4% for 2027; the $1.51trn/$1.9136trn figures are the **Spring 2026** vintage (2 Jun 2026) and are stale relative to Aug–Nov data.
- **IDC's 2026 figure ($1.29trn, +52.8%) is from 29 Apr 2026** and is far below Gartner/WSTS/JPMorgan — likely not updated for the H2-2026 memory spike. Do not mix vintages in one chart.
- **2025 base differs by source:** Gartner $809bn vs IDC $842.8bn. Growth rates are therefore not comparable across houses.
- **Custom-ASIC share numbers are secondary-sourced** (LinkedIn/Silicon Analysts summaries of TrendForce-type data). The "ASIC +44.6% vs GPU +16.1%" and "NVIDIA 75–81% of accelerator revenue" figures should be re-checked against a primary TrendForce/Counterpoint release before use in the deck.
- **Hyperscaler 2027 capex is qualitative** ("significantly", "meaningfully more") except Meta's 2026 range. No company has given a numeric 2027 capex guide as of 19 Nov 2026.
- **NVIDIA leadership note:** Q3 FY27 release quotes Colette Kress as *interim* CEO; press reports (17–18 Nov 2026) say the board has begun a CEO search. Governance item — verify before the IC meeting.
- **HBM market-share percentages** come from Counterpoint (Q1/Q2-2026) and TrendForce (2027 ranking); the two use different bases (revenue vs shipment) and are not directly comparable.
- **Auto/industrial** figures are qualitative (BNP Paribas, 23 Mar 2026); no verified 2026/2027 auto semi revenue growth number was obtained.
