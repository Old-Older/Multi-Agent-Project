## 2. Slide 11 sizing: core 50–60 / memory 15–20 / equipment 15–20 / cash 10

2.1 **Not defensible.** It is 90% risk assets with no drawdown budget, against the deck's own −38.5% in 136 days, −46% (2022) and −85% (2000–02) evidence (job3 (d)). Implied loss: ≈ −41% in a 2022 repeat, ≈ −77% in the 2000–02 tail. Neither is stated.
2.2 **Core 50–60% too high.** Semis were 19.7% of the S&P 500 at the June peak (~16% now), top-10 ≈40% of the index, NVDA ≈6% (job3 (c)). **Change to 40–45%.**
2.3 **Memory 15–20% too high.** SK hynix 3.3x / Micron 4.1x forward are peak-earnings multiples; DRAM contract growth decelerates to 0–5% QoQ by 4Q27 (TrendForce, 18 Nov 26) and Samsung P5 (2×200k wpm) plus Yongin (+300k wpm) land 2027–28. **Change to 10–12%, across ≥2 suppliers.**
2.4 **Equipment 15–20% too high.** ASML ~one-third of revenue from China (Dataconomy, 18 Nov 26), Dutch rejection of MATCH, 22.1x forward / 28.7x EV/EBITDA. **Change to 10–12%.**
2.5 **Cash/hedge 10% too low.** BofA's bear case is a 35–40% EPS cut and SOX 13.7x is still ~12% above the ~12x trough multiple (8 Nov 26). **Change to 20–25%, of which ≥5% an explicit hedge** (index puts or short SOX future), not cash.
2.6 **Proposed (sums to 100%):** core 40–45 / memory 10–12 / equipment 10–12 / ASIC-packaging 5–8 / cash 15 / hedge 5–8. Implied 2022-repeat loss ≈ −33% to −35%; 2000–02 tail ≈ −62% to −66%. Put both on the slide.
2.7 **Add a hard rule:** no single name >8% of the sleeve; total semis exposure including any S&P 500 core holding capped at a stated % of the fund (job3 bullet 3).
