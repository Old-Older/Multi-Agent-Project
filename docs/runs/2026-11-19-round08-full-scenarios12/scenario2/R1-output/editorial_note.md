# Editorial note — AI on HKUST Campuses

**Story chosen:** "AI on HKUST Campuses: The Rules Are in Your Syllabus, Not in One Rulebook" — the course-by-course GenAI policy (in force since Fall 2023-24) set against HKUST's fast-expanding AI infrastructure. Chosen because it is the AI development that most directly affects every student and every instructor, and because it is documented in official HKUST sources rather than press speculation.

**Audience:** HKUST students and faculty. Needs served: (1) what the rule actually is and where to find it; (2) whether AI detectors are used; (3) what support exists; (4) what is coming in 2026.

**Channels delivered:**
- `report_ai_on_campus.md` — the web news article (main deliverable), fact-checked PASS (see `check_report_v2.md`).
- `channels_ai_on_campus.md` — campus social/bulletin post (179 words) and a 3-minute spoken briefing script (477 words).

**Editorial judgments:**
- We did not lead on a "scandal" angle: no HKUST-specific AI-detection dispute, petition or misconduct figures could be verified, so none is reported.
- HKUST's "first university in Hong Kong" claim is attributed to HKUST, not asserted.
- The 2026 announcements (mandatory six-credit AI Common Core; AI Literacy Hub) are kept in a separate "What's coming next (2026)" section so they are not read as 2023–25 policy.
- Unverified items (Digital Education Council daily-use claim, misconduct case numbers, HKU/CUHK/PolyU comparison, faculty-attitude survey) are listed in the article's "What we could not confirm" section.

**Known gaps before publication:**
1. The briefing script is 477 spoken words, about 30 over the 400–450 target — trim Section 3 if a strict 3-minute slot is needed.
2. No interviews were conducted; the report rests on official documents and one journal abstract. A follow-up with CEI and two or three students/faculty would strengthen it.
3. The March 2023 policy circular itself is login-gated and was not read.
4. The 440-GPU SuperPOD figure is derived (55 nodes × 8), not an official count.
