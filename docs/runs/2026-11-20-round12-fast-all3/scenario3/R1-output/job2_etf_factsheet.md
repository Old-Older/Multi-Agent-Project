## 2. Tax & structural notes (HK resident, non-US person)

| Item | Fact | Source / date |
|---|---|---|
| US dividend withholding | **30%** withheld on dividends from US-domiciled ETFs (no HK–US tax treaty). Irish-domiciled UCITS equivalents (CSPX, VUAA, VUSA) suffer only **15%** at fund level under the US–Ireland treaty → ~15% saving on yield. | taxesforexpats.com, eToro, Jul 2026 / Feb 2026 |
| US estate tax | Non-resident aliens: US-situs assets (incl. US-domiciled ETFs) above **USD 60,000** exemption are exposed to US estate tax up to **40%**. UCITS (Irish) funds are non-US situs → outside this net. | StashAway, Oct 2026 |
| UCITS alternatives | S&P 500: CSPX/VUAA/VUSA; Nasdaq-100: CNDX/EQQQ; Dev. ex-US: IWDA/VEVE (global) or IDEV-type; EM: EIMI/IEMA; US small-cap value: **no clean UCITS equivalent** (ZPRV/ZPRX are US small-cap *momentum/value* blends — partial); Semis: no exact UCITS SMH clone (SMH itself is US-domiciled); T-bills: IB01/ERNA (USD T-bill UCITS). | general knowledge — ❓ not re-verified this session |

**Implication:** at ~USD 128k, US estate-tax exposure is real (only USD 60k exempt). A UCITS core (CSPX/VUAA) plus US-listed satellites for niche exposures (AVUV, SMH) is the usual compromise; alternatively keep total US-situs assets under USD 60k.
