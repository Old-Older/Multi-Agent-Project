## E. HKD/USD peg & FX for a HK investor

| Item | Figure / fact | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peg status | Linked Exchange Rate System (currency board), band HKD 7.75–7.85 per USD; monetary base 100% backed by USD assets | HKMA | 2025–26 |
| Current pressure | HKD near weak side of band; HKMA has flagged possible weak-side Convertibility Undertaking intervention as US–HK rate differential widens | AAStocks (HKMA CEO Eddie Yue); Economic Times | 6 Oct / 12 Oct 2026 |

**FX considerations:** (1) USD assets are effectively unhedged HKD exposure, but peg limits FX risk to ±~1.3% around 7.80 — no currency hedge needed for a long-horizon investor. (2) HKMA rate defence can push HKD funding costs/HIBOR up, raising the cost of leveraged or margin positions. (3) Real risk is not FX but *concentration*: HKD cash, HK property and HK salaries are already HKD/USD-bloc linked, so a 100% US book adds little diversification vs. local assets. (4) US dividend withholding tax 30% for HK residents (no treaty) and US estate tax above USD 60,000 — see ETF fact sheet.
