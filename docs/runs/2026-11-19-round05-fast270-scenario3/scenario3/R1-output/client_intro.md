# Your US Stock Plan — A Simple Introduction

**For:** a 25-year-old investor with HKD 1,000,000
**What you told us:** you can leave the money invested for 10+ years, you want the money to grow, and you can accept a temporary fall of 30–40%. No borrowing, no single stocks.
**Prices and rates in this note are as of 17–18 November 2026.**

---

## 1. What the portfolio is

We put your HKD 1,000,000 into five simple "buckets". Each bucket is a **fund (ETF)** — a basket of many companies you buy in one go, so you own a tiny slice of hundreds of businesses instead of picking a few.

| Bucket | What it holds | Your money | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Big US companies (VOO) | The 500 largest US companies | HKD 450,000 | 45% |
| 2. US tech & growth (QQQ) | The 100 largest tech-heavy US companies | HKD 150,000 | 15% |
| 3. Smaller US companies (VXF) | Smaller and mid-sized US companies | HKD 100,000 | 10% |
| 4. Companies outside the US (VXUS) | Europe, Japan, emerging markets, etc. | HKD 200,000 | 20% |
| 5. Cash buffer | Short-term US government bills (very safe, ~4% a year) | HKD 100,000 | 10% |

At today's exchange rate (USD 1 = HKD 7.8461) your HKD 1,000,000 is about **USD 127,450**.

## 2. Why it is split this way

- **Bucket 1 is the base (45%).** It is the broadest, steadiest way to own "the US economy". Most of your growth should come from here.
- **Bucket 2 adds extra growth, but only a little (15%).** You are 25 and have decades ahead, so a tech tilt makes sense. But buckets 1 and 2 both hold the same handful of giant AI and tech companies. If we put 65% into US big companies (45% + 20%), we would be buying the same few names twice over — so we trimmed this bucket to 15%. It is a side bet, not the main bet.
- **Bucket 3 adds variety (10%).** Smaller companies behave differently from the giants and are less tied to the AI theme, so they smooth the ride a little.
- **Bucket 4 spreads you outside the US (20%).** Today a handful of huge US tech companies make up a very large part of the US market. Owning other countries means you are not betting everything on those few names — this is your main protection against a US-only problem.
- **Bucket 5 is your cushion (10%).** It earns about 4% a year (3-month US government bills pay 4.33%) and gives us money to buy more shares if prices fall, without selling anything.

## 3. What could go wrong — in plain terms

**Prices can fall a lot, for a while.** Stock markets drop regularly. In a bad year this portfolio could fall about **40%**. On HKD 1,000,000 that is a **paper loss of about HKD 400,000** — you would see roughly HKD 600,000 on your statement. "Paper loss" means the loss only becomes real if you sell. History says markets have recovered from every such fall so far, but recovery can take years, which is why we only suggest this if you can wait.

**A few big tech companies matter too much.** A large part of the US market is now a small group of AI and tech giants. If they disappoint, the whole market — and buckets 1 and 2 — can fall together. That is exactly why bucket 2 is only 15%.

**Interest rates are high right now.** The 10-year US government bond pays **5.10%**, up sharply this year. When safe bonds pay that much, shares look less attractive and can be worth less. This is a real headwind for the tech-heavy part.

**The exchange rate can work against you.** Your money is in HKD but the investments are in USD. The HKD is kept between 7.75 and 7.85 per USD. Today it is 7.8461 — almost at the weak end (7.85). So the USD is unlikely to rise much further against the HKD (about 0.05% of room), but if the rate moved back to 7.75, your USD investments would be worth about **1.2% less in HKD**. That is small, but it is a real cost.

**You could panic-sell.** The biggest risk to a plan like this is selling in a bad month. If a 40% fall would keep you awake, tell us before we start.

## 4. What you need to do

1. **Invest in 3 steps, not all at once.** We put the money in over about 3 months in three "tranches": **40% first, then 30%, then 30%**, roughly one month apart. We stick to the dates whatever the market is doing — no trying to guess the best day. Money not yet invested sits in the cash buffer.
2. **Check it once a year.** Once a year we put the buckets back to the percentages above ("rebalancing" — selling a little of what grew and buying a little of what shrank). We also act sooner if a bucket drifts too far from its target: **more than 5 percentage points** for the big buckets (the 45%, 15% and 20% ones) and **more than 3 percentage points** for the smaller 10% buckets. We use the cash buffer first, so we do not have to sell shares.
3. **Leave it alone in between.** No borrowing, no single stocks, no daily checking.
4. **Tell us if your life changes** — a house, a job change, or needing the money sooner than 10 years.
