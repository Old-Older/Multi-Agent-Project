# Your Portfolio, In Plain Words

**For:** a 25-year-old Hong Kong investor · **Money:** HKD 1,000,000 · **Date:** 19 November 2026

## What we propose

| Bucket | Share | HKD | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| US broad market | 70% | 700,000 | The whole US stock market in one cheap fund (VOO + VTI) |
| Tech growth | 15% | 150,000 | The biggest US tech names (QQQ) |
| International | 10% | 100,000 | Stocks outside the US (VXUS) |
| Cash | 5% | 50,000 | Short-term US government bills (SGOV) — a buffer, not a bet |

Total yearly fund cost is about HKD 600–800 — very cheap.

## Why it fits you

You want **growth**, will not need this money for **10+ years**, and can accept a **30–40% drop** along the way.

- **Time is your friend.** Over 10+ years, markets have historically recovered from every big fall. At 25, your future salary is a second safety net.
- **You are not relying on this money soon.** That is why we can hold 95% in stocks instead of playing safe and earning little.
- **You accepted the swings.** So we do not water the plan down — we just make sure you never have to sell at the worst moment.

## Three things that could go wrong

1. **Prices can fall 30–40% and stay down for a year or two.** On HKD 1,000,000 that is about HKD 300,000–400,000 on paper. It has happened before and will again. It is not a reason to sell — it is the price of higher long-run returns.
2. **Too much sits in a few tech giants.** The biggest US tech names are roughly a third of the US market, and your tech fund adds more. If those few companies disappoint, your portfolio feels it more than the index does. That is why tech is capped at 15% and 10% goes overseas.
3. **US estate tax.** If you hold US-listed funds and pass away, the US can tax the value **above USD 60,000** at up to **40%**. If everything were bought as US-listed funds, about USD 68,000 would be exposed — potentially around HKD 190,000–210,000. **The fix we recommend:** hold the 70% core in the Ireland-domiciled versions (CSPX or VUAA) and the tech sleeve in the Ireland-domiciled Nasdaq-100 fund (CNDX). Only the international part (VXUS) and the cash part (SGOV) stay US-listed — about USD 19,000, comfortably under the USD 60,000 limit. **Decide this before you invest.**

## Three rules to follow

1. **Rebalance at ±5 percentage points.** If any bucket drifts more than 5 points from its target, bring it back. Check quarterly — not daily.
2. **Keep 5% cash plus 6 months of living expenses.** The HKD 50,000 inside the portfolio, plus 6 months of expenses held separately, so you never sell stocks to pay a bill.
3. **Invest in 4–6 equal tranches over 6–9 months.** Spreading your entry softens the risk of buying at a peak and lets you get used to ups and downs with smaller amounts first.

## One last thing

This is a **simulation, not advice** — an educational illustration built on November 2026 market data, not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Please check the numbers with a licensed adviser and your broker before any real money moves.
