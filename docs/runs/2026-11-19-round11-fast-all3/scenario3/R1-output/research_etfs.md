**Why it matters for a HK investor (non-US person):**
- **US estate tax:** US-listed ETFs are **US-situs assets**. A non-resident alien's US-situs estate above the **USD 60,000** exemption is taxed at **18–40%** (top 40%); no US–HK estate-tax treaty relief. On ~USD 128k, ~USD 68k exposed → up to ~USD 27k on death. Ireland-domiciled UCITS ETFs are **not US-situs**.
- **Dividend withholding:** US-listed ETFs pay 30% US withholding to HK holders; Ireland UCITS suffer only 15% at fund level under the US–Ireland treaty → ~15 percentage points saved; accumulating classes (CSPX/VUAA) simplify HK tax handling.
- **Trade-off:** UCITS trade in London/EU hours with wider spreads and possibly higher broker fees; US-listed ETFs are cheaper and more liquid. Common compromise: **core in UCITS, satellites US-listed**, or keep US-situs holdings under ~USD 60k.
