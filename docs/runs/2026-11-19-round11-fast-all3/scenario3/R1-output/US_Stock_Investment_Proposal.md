## 1. What we are trying to achieve
Grow HKD 1,000,000 over 10+ years by owning a low-cost, globally diversified slice of the US (and some non-US) stock market, accepting large interim swings in exchange for higher expected long-run returns. At 25 with decades of earning years ahead, the portfolio can afford to be equity-heavy; the main job is to stay invested through drawdowns and keep costs and taxes low.

## 4. Why this allocation
1. **Equity-heavy fits the brief.** 95% growth assets matches a 10+ year horizon and a stated tolerance for a 30–40% drawdown; a 25-year-old's biggest asset is future earnings, which cushions portfolio losses.
2. **Core first, cheap and broad.** 70% in US broad market (VOO 55% + VTI 15%) gives ~3,500 companies at 0.03% cost — the single most reliable way to capture US equity returns. Cost is the one variable you control: a blended ~0.07% versus a typical 1.5% fund saves roughly HKD 14,300 a year on the full HKD 1,000,000.
3. **A measured growth tilt.** 15% QQQ adds the secular tech/AI theme (10-yr 19.6% p.a. vs VOO 14.7%) without letting a single theme dominate. It is deliberately capped because the core already holds ~37% in its top 10 names.
4. **Diversification where it is cheap.** 10% VXUS buys ~8,700 non-US stocks at a much lower multiple (P/E ~14 vs ~22) — a hedge against US mega-cap de-rating, not a return forecast. (AVUV small-cap value, 0.25%, is a valid swap if you want a US-only diversifier.)
5. **5% cash is a behaviour tool, not a return play.** It funds staged entry and emergencies so you never have to sell stocks at the bottom.
6. **Staged entry over 6–9 months** (4–6 equal tranches) rather than one lump sum: it reduces the risk of buying a local peak and lets you experience volatility with smaller amounts first.

## 5. Risks of this allocation
| Risk | What it means for you | Mitigant |
|---|---|---|
| **Valuation / market risk** | High starting multiples (CAPE ~41) lower expected 10-yr returns; a broad US de-rating hits ~85% of the book (the US equity sleeves) | 10+ yr horizon; staged entry; realistic expectations (~5–7% p.a. nominal, not the last decade's 14%) |
| **Concentration risk** | Mag 7 ≈ ⅓ of the S&P 500 → ~21–24% of the portfolio via the 70% core, plus ~7% from QQQ's top-10 (≈45% of QQQ × 15%) ≈ **30% in the same mega-cap/AI names**, before counting AI-linked names outside the Mag 7 — an AI-capex shock is a portfolio event | QQQ capped at 15%; 10% international; ±5pp rebalancing bands |
| **Drawdown / behavioural risk** | A 35% fall = ~HKD 350,000 paper loss, historically lasting 12–24 months; capitulating turns it permanent | Written rules; quarterly reviews only; don't check daily. **If the fall exceeds 40%, the pre-agreed action is no change — re-read §6 and rebalance into the bands.** |
| **US estate tax (non-resident alien)** | US-listed ETFs are US-situs: up to **40% on value above USD 60,000**. | **Liquidity** | Low risk: VOO/QQQ trade tens of millions of shares daily; brief ETF discounts to NAV possible in stress | Use limit orders in stress; cash buffer avoids forced sales |

## 6. Rules to pre-commit to
1. **Rebalancing bands ±5pp:** if any sleeve drifts >5pp from target, rebalance back (quarterly check, act only on a breach).
2. **Cash buffer:** 5% (HKD 50,000) in SGOV **plus** 6 months of living expenses held outside this portfolio.
3. **Staged entry:** 4–6 equal tranches over 6–9 months, automated.
4. **Review cadence:** quarterly, not daily. No changes based on headlines.
