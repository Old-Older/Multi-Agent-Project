# Semiconductor Industry Outlook — End-2026 Perspective
## Working notes for the IC deck (fund team)

Cut-off: 18 Nov 2026. Tags: [R] reported, [E] estimate, [C] calculated.

### Spine of the argument (5 judgments)
1. **The cycle is real but it is a supply-constrained supercycle, not a normal cycle.** Memory is sold out to 2029 on LTAs; TSMC is at 66% GM; WFE is at record. The binding constraint is wafers, HBM stacks and CoWoS, not demand.
2. **2027 is the peak-growth year; 2028 is the risk year.** Hyperscaler capex growth decelerates +77% (2026) → +54% (2027) → +12% (2028) [E, GS]. Memory capex triples to ~$146bn in 2027 [E], and new DRAM capacity lands 2028. That is the classic setup for a 2028 oversupply.
3. **Value is migrating from the GPU to the memory and the packaging.** NVIDIA GM guided down to 71-72% on HBM4 cost; SK hynix printed 80% OPM. Custom ASIC share rising 27.8% → ~40% by 2030 [E] erodes NVIDIA's pricing umbrella.
4. **China is a shrinking revenue pool but a rising competitive threat.** NVIDIA China DC revenue assumed at zero; Huawei ~50% of China AI-chip market. Self-sufficiency ~30% [E]. The investable China story is equipment localisation (35% domestic share, 70% target 2027), not leading-edge logic.
5. **The portfolio answer is barbell: own the bottlenecks, underweight the commoditised middle, and buy insurance.** Bottlenecks = HBM, CoWoS/advanced packaging, leading-edge foundry, semicap. Commoditised middle = mature-node logic, commodity analog, low-end discrete.
